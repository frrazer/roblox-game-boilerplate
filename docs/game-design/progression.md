# Progression and balance

- **Progression is fast and snappy.** The player should never sit idle waiting to afford the next thing.
- **There's always a clear next goal,** and something new to unlock at every stage.
- **Ramp up activity and difficulty with progress.** Don't overwhelm new players, and don't bore experienced ones.
- **Release options one at a time** when several at once would overwhelm.
- **Make the fix for the player's current pain available early.**
- **Early rewards look exciting too.** Better things visibly look better and perform better.
- **Progress others can see** gives players something to aim for.
- **Prestige or reset systems need a clear reward and a sensible length.** They must never softlock the player, and must never wipe things players would hate to rebuild.
- **Size every number to what players can realistically earn.** Balance with a fast simulation before tuning by hand.
- **Let players see what they're buying** when luck is involved, and make sure bad outcomes don't dominate.
- **Keep upkeep light.** Maintenance turns into a chore.
- **Payouts are moments:** show them, don't just add them.
- **Check free rewards on the server** and protect them from farming.
- **Give long-term reasons to play,** such as collections, events and things to invest in.
- **Don't save what doesn't need saving.** Never break existing saves.
