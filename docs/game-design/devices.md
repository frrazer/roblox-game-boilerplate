# Devices and performance

Most players are on cheap phones and tablets, and a good share are on console. Smooth performance on your PC means nothing.

## Performance

- **Treat performance as a design constraint from day one.** Aim for 60 fps on a low-end Android.
- **Adjust quality automatically** so players never have to touch settings.
- **Core visuals work on every device.** Fancy rendering needs a fallback.
- **Avoid the known traps:** CanvasGroups, many different text sizes (they cause flicker), large numbers of Humanoids, and high part counts.
- **Cross-server systems hold up** when big player spikes hit.

## Mobile

- **Landscape only.**
- **Action buttons look like Roblox's own and sit beside the jump button.** Make them big, evenly sized, and clear of each other.
- **Give frequent actions their own buttons;** tapping on things in the world is imprecise.
- **Anything you drag, place or aim works comfortably by touch.**
- **On PC, show a key hint** instead of an on-screen button.

## Console

- **Every action and menu works with a controller.**

## Checking

- **Test touch and controller input in Studio's device emulator.** When the user is around, ask them to try it on a real phone.
