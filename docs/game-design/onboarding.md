# The first minute

Most players decide whether to stay within the first minute. The goal: within 10 to 30 seconds, they care and are doing the thing the title and thumbnail promised.

- **Start with the promise.** Put players straight into the core action. Remove every setup step that stands between joining and the fun.
- **Lead with the best mechanic,** not everything at once.
- **Make them care before you teach** when the game has stakes.
- **Teach with actions and visuals, not text:** let the level show the way, highlight what to touch, and keep any objective to one short line.
- **Teach one thing at a time,** and cover every action the core loop needs.
- **Never mention a system or term the player hasn't been shown.** Teach a hidden system with a tiny lesson the moment it first matters.
- **Never ask for something that isn't possible yet.** No fail states or surprises in the tutorial.
- **Turn guidance off** as soon as the action is learned.
- **No waiting.**
- **Nothing else competes with the tutorial.** No shop offers, popups or rewards until it's over, then one at a time.
- **The first reward is exciting,** and gives a glimpse of what's possible later.
- **Give them a reason to come back tomorrow.**
- **Track each tutorial step as a funnel step** so drop-off can be measured.
- **Check the first minute on mobile and console too.**
