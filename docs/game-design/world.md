# World

In the top games the map is the progress bar, the shops are places you walk past, and information sits on the object it describes. Names in brackets are evidence.

## Map layout as progression

- **Lay progress out in space, ordered by difficulty.** Two shapes dominate:
  - **One straight line from home outward** (9 of 16 games). Examples: a ~12,000-stud corridor of 13–15 stages [Keyboard Escape, Spider Arm]; a 5,000–7,000-stud strip of biome zones [Break and Steal, Steal An Egg]; a 2,700-stud street of rarity zones [Scare Your Neighbor]; a throwing canyon [Throw Food]; an endless highway [Steal and Drive]; a vertical shaft you descend [Depth Incremental].
  - **A hub with spokes** where difficulty rises toward or away from the centre. In [Mini War], 6 plots sit on a ring and each has its own road to the centre. A bandit camp, a garrison and military bases line that road, and the city at the centre is shared by everyone. In [Bug Evolution], 7 zones radiate from a starter meadow, each labelled with a level hint (Lv 1+, 8+ … 40+).
- **Make distance equal difficulty and value.** Better zones are further away, and later zones are longer (93 → 900 studs [Break and Steal], 93 → 1,090 [Steal An Egg]). So the escape gets longer exactly as the chaser gets faster. Inside a zone, put the best loot deepest [Break and Steal] or let the rarest roll only in the last 10% of a biome [Throw Food].
- **Put home at one end, so the escape direction is never in doubt.** Then the tutorial arrow can simply point to the nearest safe edge [Break and Steal].
- **Let players see what's ahead.** The next biomes rise behind the hub wall [Break and Steal]. The lane recedes to a glowing portal [Steal An Egg]. You can see down the canyon into colours you haven't reached [Throw Food]. A "(World 2)" portal sign promises more [Spider Arm]. Mystery works too: only the next step exists and the shaft below is empty [Depth Incremental].
- **Give each zone its own identity:** its own floor and wall palette and props, a ground tint in the rarity colour, houses that grow bigger per tier, a giant guard and a name banner sliding in as you cross [Scare Your Neighbor, Break and Steal, Steal An Egg]. Change the lighting preset and ambient sound per layer [Depth Incremental]. A player should know where they are from one screenshot.
- **Wall off linear courses** so nobody gets lost. Use a 160-stud-wide walled corridor [Spider Arm], or 80-stud earth walls along the street [Scare Your Neighbor]. In open maps, use ridges with deliberate gaps to make choke points and hunting lanes, and keep safe zones near the start [Bug Evolution].
- **Make travel cheap once you're deep.** A teleport menu lists every layer and room as a card with a screenshot [Depth Incremental]. Use stage checkpoints [Keyboard Escape] and "BACK TO BASE" with a 3D pin [Steal and Drive].

## The hub and the plot

- **Spawn facing the activity, with everything you need within ~80 studs** [Spider Arm].
- **Frame the path with what you can't afford yet.** Shops and trainers line the path to the climb, so every lap passes the next armature and the rig you haven't unlocked [Spider Arm]. Paid crate pads sit on the only road between the garage and the start line [Backseat Rally]. Leaderboards and the SELL stand sit at the head of the canyon, so you pass them on every throw [Throw Food].
- **Plots:** 5–8 per server, side by side or around a hub, roughly 45×45 to 160×160 studs, all visible to each other. Put a name sign with headshot over each.
- **Make the building the production line.** Crates come in the back door, cross the cleaning and framing benches, and end up on the gallery wall [Clean a Painting]. Keep the loop a short walk: all stations within a few steps (16-stud interact range) [My Small Business], or ~75 studs from fish pool to deposit pad [Sushi].
- **Grow the plot in place.** Locked rooms are taped outlines on concrete you can walk on [My Small Business]. The pier extends 20 studs per row [Sushi]. The gallery rebuilds into a museum [Clean a Painting]. A second floor adds 6 bays [Steal and Drive].
- **Keep session-game lobbies small and readable:** 80×114 studs [DONT SPLASH], 185×185 [Criminal Sim].
  - Queue by walking into the thing that takes you to the run, like the getaway truck or a doorway pad. Show its state from across the yard with sign text and light strips: green "OPEN - STEP INSIDE", yellow "HOST IS SETTING UP...", red "FULL" [Criminal Sim]. Floor squares go yellow when empty and green when a party is waiting [DONT SPLASH].
  - Show the threat or the run from the lobby: a live peek of the pool through each doorway, and the monster in an aquarium [DONT SPLASH].
  - Give waiting players something to do, like a small obby that pays $250 once a day [Criminal Sim].

## Information in the world

Put each fact on the object it's about, so the player never opens a menu to decide.

- **Requirements on every gate.** "STAGE 2 😐 Level 8 recommended", with the emoji getting angrier per stage [Keyboard Escape]. "STAGE 1 / Recommended Level 0" and "HOLD SPACE TO CLIMB" painted on the first wall [Spider Arm]. "YOUR SCARE CHANCE 42%" with a coloured bar: red under 25%, then orange, then yellow, green at 75%+ [Scare Your Neighbor]. A sign with the required speed in each room [Steal An Egg].
- **Prices on objects.** Show the price on the next ghost step, green if affordable and red if not, or a reason ("Needs Rebirth 2", "Defeat the Worm Queen") [Depth Incremental]. Crates on the ground carry "CRATE 25 / $8T" in rarity colour [Clean a Painting]. Pads glow green, red or grey by affordability [Clean a Painting, Sushi]. Show a floating "!" when the cash option is affordable [Scare Your Neighbor].
- **Labels on owned things.** Show name, rarity, mutation, size and $/s: "GOLD Lion BIG $3.11K/s" [Throw Food]. Show a bill tag over customers [Sushi]. Show "WORTH $X" plus a like counter on your dealership [Steal and Drive].
- **Explain systems on boards.** A market board shows the current fish value and a 12-point chart, green above 1.0x and red below [Sushi]. Cork boards answer "HOW TO START A JOB" and "PICK YOUR JOB" right before the trucks [Criminal Sim]. A price board shows each good's % change and a "Next Price In 01:22" timer [Mini War].
- **Countdowns where the event happens.** "CRATE DELIVERY 02:47", pulsing each second for the last 10 s [Clean a Painting]. A countdown on the wall that blocks entry during the night reset [Steal An Egg]. "Opens at 5 wins" on a faded portal [Keyboard Escape].
- **Use lines on the floor:** a glowing throw line to walk across, white lines between sub-areas [Throw Food], and a red line at the danger edge [Break and Steal].
- **Leaderboards as physical boards** with podium statues of the top 3 [Criminal Sim, Backseat Rally]. Rank income per second, not total money, to reward building [Scare Your Neighbor].
- **Push information into the world and keep the HUD near empty.** In [Backseat Rally] the speedometer is off; gauges, a trip meter, a tablet and a swinging air freshener live in the cockpit. The centre of the screen should show the game.

## Shops in the world

- **Walk-in stands open when you step into the ring and close when you leave** (~18 studs) [Scare Your Neighbor, Criminal Sim]. If the player closes one manually, don't reopen it until they step out and back in, so it never feels pushy [Scare Your Neighbor].
- **Use pads you step or drive onto:** hire pads [Sushi], drive-in crate pads with glowing walls, a 3D crate on a plinth and a billboard hook such as "WIN THE BRENNER S1 · R$299" [Backseat Rally].
- **Put the paid version beside the free version.** Free and paid treadmills sit on one dais, and stepping onto a paid one prompts the purchase [Keyboard Escape]. A purple x2 win pad sits next to the free yellow one at every stage top [Spider Arm].
- **Display flagship items:** a spotlit turntable showroom for the pass car [Backseat Rally], mannequins wearing the bundle's title and aura [Criminal Sim], full-size models on pedestals with price plates [Spider Arm].
- **Put upgrade signs on the thing they upgrade**, with $ and R$ buttons side by side [Scare Your Neighbor, Steal An Egg]. A pass board on your own pier [Sushi].
- **Theme menus as objects:** a laptop [Criminal Sim], a clipboard "Expand" menu [My Small Business], a market stall [DONT SPLASH].
- **Where games disagree:** [Sushi] and [Backseat Rally] sell almost only through the world, while most others pair world shops with a HUD shop button. Do both: the world is for discovery and desire, the HUD is for quick access.

## Readability and look

- **Chunky, saturated, toy-like** is the dominant style: stud textures on every surface and blocky low-poly props, as in 10+ games. Typical post-processing:
  - ColorCorrection saturation +0.1 to +0.3 (up to +0.5 [Sushi]), contrast +0.05 to +0.18.
  - Bloom 0.25–0.55, with a high threshold so only neon glows [Throw Food].
  - Atmosphere density 0.25–0.31.
- **Bright midday or warm late afternoon reads best on phones.** One game turns GlobalShadows off for flat readability [Throw Food]. If the scene is dark, light it with neon, fireflies and glowing pickups [Bug Evolution, Depth Incremental].
- **Make valuable things look different from the world.** Realistic cars against a toy-like world [Steal and Drive]. Real paintings in a cartoon gallery [Clean a Painting].
- **Let scale sell the fantasy.** An ant is ~1×3 studs and flowers are 24 studs tall, so you feel tiny, then visibly outgrow the meadow [Bug Evolution].
- **Use rarity colours consistently** in the world and the UI: ground tint, outlines, labels, podium glow.
- **Keep the camera's view clear.** The roof fades to 0.85 transparency when you're inside [Sushi]. Everything reads from a high camera when looking across the map at armies [Mini War].
- **Keep the world alive.** Bobbing and spinning props [DONT SPLASH]. A day/night cycle with keyframed lighting and lanterns that fade on [Sushi]. Weather. NPCs walking through the space.
- **Mind performance.** Stream far stages in and keep them out of the save [Keyboard Escape]. Use LOD and plot culling, and adapt quality to FPS [Break and Steal, Mini War].
