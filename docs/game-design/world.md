# World

## Map

- **Give the fantasy places to go.** A game with nowhere to go feels flat.
- **Size the space for how players move through it.** It shouldn't be cramped, and it shouldn't be empty. Tighter usually beats bigger.
- **The ground has some shape.** Perfectly flat looks unfinished.
- **Put things where they logically belong,** next to what they relate to.

## Builds and props

- **It holds up when someone looks closely.** Nothing floats, clips, z-fights or faces the wrong way, and nothing is left untextured.
- **Build props properly or leave them out.** Crude stand-ins look worse than nothing.
- **Vary repeated things.** Never place identical copies in rigid grids.
- **Better tiers look clearly different,** not just recoloured.
- **Scale stays consistent.**
- **Anything that looks interactive is interactive,** or obviously decoration.
- **Interactions never break the world.**
- **Keep one art style.** Use no free models that clash with it, and only assets we're allowed to use.
- **Custom characters look native to Roblox.**
- **Detailed 3D models** (characters, props, vehicles) are made outside Studio, in Blender or as generated meshes, and brought in with `t3_game_import_model`. Check the size it reports and fix the scale.
- **Hand-built models stay in Studio,** outside Rojo, so syncing can't overwrite them.

## Lighting

- **Comfortable lighting:** neither blinding nor too dark. Moody is fine if players can still see.
- **Use a modern Atmosphere,** not the legacy fog properties.
- **No dark patches** in play areas.

## NPCs and enemies

- **They behave believably** and are animated naturally.
- **Anything the player hires or upgrades does its job visibly, straight away.**
- **Spawn things out of players' view,** never on top of them, and spread them sensibly.
