# Feel

"Satisfying" is the goal. A mechanic that works but has no feel isn't finished.

## Actions

- **The main action feels physical,** and its response scales with how hard, fast or well the player did it.
- **Show change happening.** When something transforms, the player watches it change; it never just swaps to a new model.
- **Every hit and reward is visible** on screen.
- **Movement is fast and responsive** enough for the fantasy.
- **Say why an action failed.** A silent failure looks like a bug.
- **Frame the focus of attention.** Whatever the player is working on is large, clear and facing the camera.
- **Make targets easy to see** against every background.
- **Completion matches what the player sees.** If it looks done, it's done.
- **Every input should count.** Avoid slow, spammable or passive "hold to do it" steps.
- **Players keep control:** their own cursor, their own movement, their own choices.
- **Nothing pops in.** Preload what's about to appear.

## Animation

- **Motion is believable.** Things move the way players expect, and nothing clips through anything.
- **Transitions blend.** No snaps, teleports or one-frame gaps between states.
- **Movement is smooth.** No jitter or robotic turning.
- **Groups of identical things vary,** so a crowd doesn't move in lockstep.
- **Keep timing tight.** Cut beats and flashes that slow things down.

## Big moments

- **Big moments get time and build-up,** with a clear beginning, middle and end. Don't rush them.
- **Push the payoff further** than feels necessary, but never so far that it blinds the screen.
- **Sync big moments to the music** where there is music.
- **Never double up an effect** or a sound.
- **Tween between moods** rather than cutting harshly.

## Sound and haptics

- **A silent game feels unfinished.** Plan a full set of sounds: UI, actions, rewards, progression, ambience and music.
- **You can't hear, so don't choose sounds blind.** List the sounds needed, with search terms, and let the user choose. Balance the volumes by analysing the files.
- **Add haptics** on mobile and controller for exciting moments.

## Tone

- **Characters and text have personality and variety,** pitched at a young audience without being cringe.
