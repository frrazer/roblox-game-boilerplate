# Monetisation

The best products relieve a pain the player already feels, can be bought again and again, and appear exactly where and when that pain is felt. Gameplay always comes first.

## What to sell

- **Painkillers, not nice-to-haves.** For example: "I don't want to grind", "I don't want to wait", "I don't want to lose my stuff", "I want to show off".
- **Prefer consumables (developer products)** to game passes for anything that can be bought repeatedly.
- **Offer three sizes,** and always include a cheap starter option.
- **Show the best possible outcome** of what they're buying.
- **Price repeat purchases of the same advantage on a rising scale,** so nobody can simply buy the top spot.

## Where and when

- **Offer it at the moment of need.**
- **Give players several ways to buy,** not just one shop button.
- **Put products where players already go.**
- **Pause timers while a purchase prompt is open.**

## Rules

- **Never pay-to-win.**
- **Protect the economy.** Make sure progression accounts for anything you sell.
- **No shop during onboarding.**
- **Never hardcode Robux prices.** Read them from MarketplaceService.
- **Name products in plain words.**
- **Judge products on retention as well as Robux.** Revert any product that hurts play time or retention.
