# Monetisation

The top games sell a lot without feeling like cash grabs. They sell relief at the moment of pain, scale every pack to the player, put a free path next to every paid one, and keep the shop out of the first minutes. Names in brackets are evidence.

## What to sell

| Product type | What it solves | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Chore removers** (passes) | A task the player has done by hand many times | Auto Clean R$100 [Clean a Painting]; Cashier R$24 auto-collect → Manager R$400 double catch [Sushi]; Auto Bubbles R$149 [Spider Arm]; "Strong Back **for good**" [Criminal Sim] |
| **Core multipliers** | Grind speed | x2 Cash/Money passes R$60–320; permanent ×2 ladders sold as developer products (see price ladders) |
| **Next tier now** | Saving up | A Robux button beside the cash price on every tier card [Break and Steal, Scare Your Neighbor, Steal An Egg, Mini War] |
| **Timer skips** | Waiting | Skips priced by time remaining [Clean a Painting, Mini War, Break and Steal] |
| **Rescues** | Losing a run at the frontier | Revive R$19–29 [Keyboard Escape, Spider Arm, Bug Evolution]; self-rescue R$19 [DONT SPLASH]; skip stage R$49 [Spider Arm] |
| **Stat packs** | "I can't escape this zone" | Speed packs sized to the gap [Break and Steal, Steal An Egg] |
| **Luck and gacha** | Collection | Limited eggs or crates in packs of 1/3/10/50 with odds shown and pity [Steal An Egg, Mini War, Backseat Rally] |
| **Social boosts** | Status | A 15-min server boost that names the buyer, R$199 [Spider Arm, Keyboard Escape]; Server Luck [Steal An Egg] |
| **Starter pack** | First purchase | R$9–49, once, time-limited (see below) |
| **VIP** | Committed players | +10% to +50% income, a chat tag and a gold sign, R$80–320 [Bug Evolution, Spider Arm, Steal and Drive] |
| **Creative tools** | Self-expression | Brush packs, colour wheel, box designer [My Small Business] |
| **Flex** | Being seen | Titles with auras [Criminal Sim]; a car radio nearby players can hear (that game's first pass) [Steal and Drive] |

- **Price passes by the friction they remove.** Cheap passes automate walking chores; expensive ones multiply output [Sushi].
- **Sell in-run upgrades as permanent "for good" versions.** Free players rebuy them with cash every run, and payers keep them [Criminal Sim].
- **Add a gift button beside every price** (most games), with a friend picker and "gets it next time they join" for offline friends [Depth Incremental].

## Where and when to offer it

**Offer at the moment of pain, sized to fix it.**
- Caught by the guard → 0.5 s later a speed pack slides in for 10 s. It's the **smallest pack that covers your exact shortfall** [Break and Steal, Steal An Egg].
- Grip under 30% mid-climb → a pulsing "REFILL R$25 [R]" button on the grip bar [Spider Arm].
- Another player starts eating you → "+50 LEVELS NOW → LV 73" for R$9, shown for 7 s, on a 90 s cooldown [Bug Evolution].
- Grabbed by the monster → "BUY SELF-RESCUE R$19". The 15 s timer **pauses while checkout is open** [DONT SPLASH].
- Wrecked → revive with an 8–10 s timer [Steal and Drive, Spider Arm]. After 2 falls on one stage, add "Skip Stage R$49" [Spider Arm].
- Can't afford something → open the shop on the coins tab, or offer the smallest pack that covers the gap [My Small Business, Mini War]. "NEED $X MORE" with a red shake [Steal and Drive].
- A tool shows "NONE LEFT" → a refill button [DONT SPLASH]. The catalog is 75% full → offer Bigger Catalog. A print has more than 20 s left → offer Faster Printer [My Small Business].

**Offer at the moment of payoff.**
- On the rewards screen, add a struck-through ghost line, "WITH VIP ~~+X CR~~", showing exactly what you missed [Backseat Rally].
- On the victory card, "Run coins 570 + 570 = 1140" for Double Run Coins [DONT SPLASH].
- On the welcome-back screen, offer 2x offline earnings [Throw Food, Steal and Drive, Break and Steal].

**Offer at the moment of anticipation.** "Super Lucky Throw" (R$9, best of 30 rolls) appears while the throw meter is running, once you have 15+ throws [Throw Food].

**Put offers in the world.** Stepping on a locked paid treadmill opens its purchase prompt [Keyboard Escape]. Paid training rigs stand next to the free one [Spider Arm]. You drive onto crate pads on the only road to the start line [Backseat Rally]. Standing still on a bundle mannequin's ring for 0.35 s opens the offer [Criminal Sim]. Upgrade signs carry a $ button and an R$ button [Scare Your Neighbor]. In-world rainbow orbs offer "+30 LEVELS" when touched [Bug Evolution]. See world.md.

**Show only the next rung**, at its cheapest-feeling price: "2x Speed ONLY 4" [Keyboard Escape], "OP! x2 Train ONLY R$3" with a bouncing arrow [Spider Arm].

**Pace the offers.**
- Never during the tutorial or its quiet period (see onboarding.md).
- Leave ≥240 s between any two offers and ≥480 s between two of the same kind, back off ×1.5 each time up to 1,200 s, and make each offer at most twice. Never show one over a window or cinematic [Throw Food].
- Show at most 2 pack offers, with a 1-hour rest between them. Rotate one pass ad: 60 s on, 5 min off [My Small Business].
- Only offer a revive past stage 6, at or beyond your best stage, and A/B test how often: every death, every 4th or every 10th [Keyboard Escape].
- Never stack popups, and auto-dismiss them [Bug Evolution].

**Target by spending history.** Offer the R$160 Bunny goodie only to players who've spent ≥R$200. Offer the R$16 Mini Pack only to returning non-spenders after the starter pack expires [My Small Business]. Hide the R$1,360 "armored truck every night" pass until level 30, when its value is clear [Criminal Sim].

**Give free trials.** Holographic for 5 min at L5 and x2 Coins for 10 min at L9: the player feels the boost, then loses it [My Small Business].

## Price ladders

- **Impulse entry, R$3–9:** the first x2 rung at R$3 [Spider Arm, Scare Your Neighbor]; a R$9 starter kit [Criminal Sim]; Super Throw R$9 [Throw Food]; +50 levels R$9 [Bug Evolution]. R$3 turns a player into a payer.
- **Permanent ×2 ladders** (each rung doubles a permanent multiplier):
  - R$3, 20, 49, 99, 139, 199, 259, 599, 1,299 [Spider Arm]
  - R$3, 9, 20, 32, 64, 104, 160, 240, 400, up to ×512 [Scare Your Neighbor]
- **Rescues R$19–29.** Escalate within a run: R$25 / 45 / 75 [Steal and Drive].
- **Passes:** typically R$40–400. "OP" passes R$799–1,360 sit in the world as aspiration [Spider Arm, Criminal Sim].
- **Starter pack: R$9–49, once, on a timer** (15 min [Mini War], 30 min [My Small Business], 45 min [Depth Incremental], 24–48 h [Bug Evolution, Criminal Sim]). Anchor it against computed worth: "-89%", worth R$89 crossed out, where R$89 is the sum of the real item prices [Criminal Sim]. Follow it with a "LAST CHANCE R$9" tile you can dismiss.
- **Packs:** steps of ~2–3x in price, with value per Robux climbing steeply ($50/R$ at the bottom, $521/R$ at the top [Criminal Sim]). Tag them POPULAR / BEST VALUE / BEST DEAL.
- **Decoys:**
  - Six single "Max X" upgrades at R$150 each next to "All Upgrades Maxed" at R$149 [DONT SPLASH].
  - Revive + boost R$30, plain revive R$19, or free with an ad, on a 10 s timer [Keyboard Escape].
  - A bundle of every pass, marked "SAVE 20%" [Depth Incremental].
- **Gacha packs:** 1 / 3 / 10 / 50 for R$19 / 179 / 879 / 1,279, with a struck-through "5,959" on the 50 [Steal An Egg]. Bundles save 10% / 20% [Backseat Rally].
- **Time-priced skips** [Clean a Painting]: R$19 for ≤2 min of saving, then 35 (≤5 min), 59 (≤15 min), 89 (≤30 min), 129 (≤1 h), 179 (≤2 h), 249 (≤4 h), 349 (≤8 h), 449 (≤16 h), 749 (≤24 h), 999 beyond that.
- **Fetch live prices** and show "..." until they load [DONT SPLASH, Criminal Sim]. Several games show live prices below their config prices, so they price-test.

## Dynamic sizing

**Never sell a flat amount of currency.** A fixed $10K pack is worthless an hour in. Every top game scales packs to the player:

- **Minutes of your own income:** 2.5 / 15 / 30 / 90 min [Throw Food]; 40 min, 2 h, 6 h, 18 h, 48 h of peak income, with floors, re-quoted only upward [Steal An Egg]; `max(floor, income/min × 15/30/60/120)` [Mini War].
- **Runs of your best stage:** 1.2 / 4 / 11 / 30 runs, with floors of 12 / 40 / 110 / 300 [Spider Arm].
- **Share of your current stat:** speed packs at 2%–120% of current Speed, rounded to "nice" steps (1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 10) [Keyboard Escape].
- **Tied to the next goal:** cash packs based on the price of the next crate tier [Clean a Painting]; by highest world [Steal and Drive]; `max(minimum, progress × share)` [My Small Business].
- **Scaled to your best item:** an egg gives an animal worth 110%–1,200% of your best animal's income [Throw Food].
- **Exactly the gap:** the smallest pack that clears the shortfall [Break and Steal, Steal An Egg, Mini War, Throw Food]. A revive's speed boost is sized to the gap to the stage's recommended speed, +10 to +30 [Keyboard Escape].
- Round amounts to nice numbers. A/B test the sizing [Steal An Egg].

## Fairness and trust

- **Show odds on every random item**, show pity progress ("Epic or better in 3", "Guaranteed Limited: 0/25"), convert duplicates to currency, and say when a pool is complete [Backseat Rally, Mini War, Depth Incremental].
- **Respect policy.** Hide paid random items where `PolicyService` restricts them [Sushi, Throw Food].
- **Put a cash path and a Robux path on the same card.** Paying then reads as "skip the wait", never "the only way".
- **Protect buyers.**
  - Confirm before selling a Robux item ("You paid R$X") [Throw Food].
  - "Cancelled. You weren't charged." [DONT SPLASH]
  - "Still loading what you own" instead of allowing a rebuy [Criminal Sim].
  - Refuse redundant server-luck buys [Steal An Egg].
  - Pause timers during checkout, and carry credits over if the target is gone [DONT SPLASH].
- **Let owners switch passes off.** Owned employees have an ON/OFF toggle, so buying never removes a choice [Sushi].
- **Never let money beat new players.** Use PvP shields (see designing.md).
- **Make buying feel good.** Dim the screen to 55% with rotating golden rays behind the purchase prompt [Depth Incremental]. Show confetti with "Thank you so much!" [Break and Steal]. Show "YOURS!" with icons popping [Criminal Sim].
- **Where games disagree:** griefing as a product. Paid pushes knock players into the water, and the splash attracts the monster [DONT SPLASH]. Paying to steal another player's production [Mini War]. Only sell this when the target has counterplay and it creates fun moments, not just losses.

## Free alternatives

- **Every paid rescue needs a free path.** A free revive every 60 min with a live countdown [Bug Evolution]. Teammates can pull you out [DONT SPLASH]. Revives can be earned from an ad or from tokens [Keyboard Escape, DONT SPLASH]. One free Lucky Roll per day [Clean a Painting].
- **Rewarded video ads** for small boosts: a 10-min x2 [Keyboard Escape], skip a delivery [Clean a Painting], +5 min growth [Steal An Egg], 3 free potions a day [Break and Steal].
- **Let the free version create the want.** Free bubbles sit next to a "MISSED 3 – Auto Bubbles R$149" counter [Spider Arm]. Free hourly abilities show "Use Now" for Robux while on cooldown [Sushi].
- **Use free gifts to teach where the shop is.** A "FREE" tag appears on the shop after 4 throws; the free animal is worth 8x your best [Throw Food]. A FREE tab puts free rewards directly above a row of R$19–29 "CHEAP DEALS" [Spider Arm].
- **Label free rewards with their Robux worth** (a daily tile shows "99") to teach prices [Mini War].

## Never do

- Show a shop or offer before the first win.
- Use flat-amount packs.
- Put every offer only in a menu.
- Pop a revive on every death.
- Make R$99+ the first thing a player sees.
- Hide odds or penalties.
- Stack popups.
