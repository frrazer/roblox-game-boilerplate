# Packaging: title, thumbnail, description, trailer

Players see the thumbnail first, but the title gives it meaning. Write the title first.

## Title

- **Write at a third-grade reading level.**
- **Use proven patterns:** verb + noun ("Grow a Garden"), "Be a ___", "Escape the ___".
- **Hint at the challenge or the fantasy.**
- **Research keywords.** A keyword is strong when *several* games that use it have huge visit counts.
- **No clickbait.** The first minute has to deliver what the title promises.

## Thumbnail

- **Show what the player will feel,** and what the game actually is.
- **Keep it simple:** one punchline, one or two characters with readable expressions, a clean background and strong contrast.
- **Use classic Roblox avatars,** placed naturally in the scene rather than pasted on top.
- **Use little or no text.**
- **It must not look like AI slop:** no artefacts, no impossible physics, no over-the-top drama. Real Roblox materials help.
- **Learn from reference thumbnails without copying their content.**
- **Compose the shot in Studio first,** and show it before any AI enhancement.
- **Check it reads on a phone.**
- **Make several variants** for testing.

## Description

- **Short and scannable:** a "Welcome to X!" opener and a few emoji bullets of features.

## Trailer

- **Use the `trailer` skill.**
